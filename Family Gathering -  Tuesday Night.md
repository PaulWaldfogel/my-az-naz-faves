📖 Sermon Summary: "Holiness in a Secular Age"

**Speaker:** Dr. Tim Gaines ([31:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=1874s))  
**Scripture Focus:** Isaiah 6:1–8 ([34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2085s))

1. Encountering the Transcendent

Dr. Gaines opened by reading Isaiah's vision of the Lord in the temple twice, asking the congregation to imagine the sensory terror, wonder, and awe of facing a holy God ([35:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2111s)). He emphasized that Isaiah's mission did not come from self-reconfiguration, but from being completely undone and remade by a God who cannot be contained ([42:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2527s)).

2. Defining the "Secular Age"

He outlined three historical shifts in human culture and secularity ([43:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2621s)):

- **Secular 1 (The Middle Ages):** Believing in God was foundational ([43:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2637s)). A baseline distinction existed between sacred vocations (priests) and secular vocations (butchers, bakers), with no inherent rebellion intended ([44:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2666s)).
- **Secular 2 (The Public Sphere):** The creation of religiously neutral spaces, where personal faith is permitted but required to stay "undercover" or strictly outside the classroom ([44:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2699s)).
- **Secular 3 (Modern Pluralism):** God is valid, but is treated as just one equal option among many competing ideas (e.g., placing Jesus on the exact same identity footing as dance or chocolate) ([45:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=2734s)).

3. The Danger of "Imminentizing" God

In a secular frame, meaning is no longer sought in the transcendent realm above us ([52:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=3166s)). Instead, people try to custom-build meaning using everyday materials (careers, spouses, objects) and simply "add Jesus" to the mix ([54:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=3261s)). Using a humorous analogy about eating nothing but cafeteria pie, he warned that reducing God to a personal identity accessory ultimately leaves people deeply bored, endlessly reconfiguring the same empty materials of life ([57:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=3441s)).

> **Key Takeaway:** Holiness is not legalism or simply avoiding bad behaviors ([1:02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=3751s)). True holiness means recognizing the vibrant, transcendent wonder of a completely "other" God ([1:02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxC7I0wQFoE&t=3755s)).
